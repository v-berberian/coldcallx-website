How to call a list of phone numbers on iPhone

By Vadym Berberian · Cold Call X · September 2026 · 6 min read

The short answer: iPhone has no built-in way to call a list. The Phone app dials one number at a time, and nothing in iOS takes a spreadsheet of leads and works down it for you. Tap-to-call links and a Shortcuts loop get you part of the way; past a few dozen numbers, a power dialer app does the job properly. Here are the four ways, what each one is good for, and how to set up the one that scales.

Four ways to call a list of numbers on an iPhone

1. Tap-to-call links

Paste the numbers into an email to yourself and iOS turns them into links you can tap to call. In Google Sheets, a HYPERLINK formula with a tel: link does the same for a whole column. It's free, and it's fine for twenty calls. For two hundred, every call becomes find the row, tap, call, come back, type the note, and nothing reminds you who asked for a callback on Thursday.

2. Favorites and Contacts

The Phone app's Favorites are one tap away, and they're the right tool for the ten people you call every week. For a lead list they're the wrong one: adding 300 prospects to Contacts buries your real contacts on every device your address book syncs to, and Contacts has no idea whom you've already called or what they said.

3. A Shortcuts loop

The Shortcuts app has a Call action and a Repeat with Each loop, so you can build a shortcut that works through a list of numbers. What it can't do is the part that makes a call list useful: it doesn't know whether anyone answered, has nowhere to record an outcome or a note, and doesn't schedule the next attempt. It's a fine weekend project and a poor way to run a prospecting session.

4. A power dialer app

A power dialer app imports the list, keeps it out of your personal contacts, and dials down it one lead at a time: when a call ends, you tap what happened and the next number is ready. That's what Cold Call X does. It's what many people search for as an "auto dialer for iPhone", but it's a power dialer: every call goes through the iPhone's own phone app, one at a time, from your own number, with you on the line.

How to call a list with Cold Call X, step by step

  1. Get the list in. Import a CSV or Excel export, or connect Google Sheets, HubSpot or Salesforce (beta). Columns are mapped automatically, and a lead with a mobile and an office number stays one lead with two numbers. The list lives in the app, not in your Contacts.
  2. Set your guardrails. Turn on Calling Hours so leads are only dialed during their own daytime, in any time zone, and set a daily dial goal. Anyone on your Do Not Call list is blocked in every list and every future import.
  3. Tap Call. Cold Call X hands the number to the iPhone's phone app, and the call goes out from your own number over your carrier. Every lead has its notes, a call script and objection cards.

What no iPhone app can do

iOS doesn't let a third-party app place calls silently in the background, so every call goes through the system phone app, one at a time. That rules out predictive and parallel dialing, where software rings several numbers at once and connects you to whoever answers; that needs cloud telephony and rented numbers. For one person working a list, one live call at a time from your own number is also the pattern that looks like normal calling to a carrier, and the cautious one legally. Before you dial: keep the list clean and legal

  • Scrub the list first. In the US, sales calls to consumers must skip numbers on the National Do Not Call Registry unless an exemption applies (47 CFR 64.1200(c)(2)); businesses get access through the FTC's registry site for telemarketers. Cold Call X's Do Not Call list is a personal suppression list: it blocks anyone you add, across every list, but it doesn't check national registries.
  • Call inside the window. Federal rules allow telephone solicitations to homes only between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. in the called person's local time (47 CFR 64.1200(c)(1)), and some states are stricter. Calling Hours does the time-zone math; our calling hours guide lists the windows for the US, Canada, the UK and Australia.
  • Say who you are, and honor "don't call me". A telemarketing call must give the caller's name, the business's name and a number or address where it can be reached, and a do-not-call request must be honored within 10 business days (47 CFR 64.1200(d)). Add anyone who asks to your Do Not Call list on the spot.

Outside the US the rules differ: see cold calling in the UK and in Australia, and the auto dialer laws guide for consent and recorded messages. This is general information, not legal advice.

Calling a list on iPhone, answered

Can an iPhone call a list of numbers automatically?+

Not by itself. iOS has no built-in way to import a list and dial down it. A power dialer app such as Cold Call X does: import the list, tap Call, and when each call ends you tap the outcome and the next lead is ready, at the pace you choose, down to a 0-second Rocket mode. Every call still goes through the iPhone's own phone app, one at a time, from your own number.

How do I import a list of phone numbers to call on my iPhone?+

Export the list as a CSV or Excel file, or keep it in Google Sheets, HubSpot or Salesforce. Cold Call X imports any of them and maps the columns automatically, so the name, company and every phone number land on the right lead. The list stays in the app instead of filling up your personal contacts.

Can I use iPhone Shortcuts to call a list of numbers?+

You can build one: the Shortcuts app has a Call action and a Repeat with Each loop. What a shortcut can't do is the part that makes a call list useful. It doesn't know whether anyone answered, has nowhere to record an outcome or a note, and doesn't schedule the next attempt, so it suits a handful of numbers, not a prospecting list.

Will the calls come from my own number?+

With Cold Call X, yes. Each call goes out through the iPhone's own phone app on your number and your carrier plan, so there is no VoIP line or second number to rent and callbacks ring your phone. Most cloud dialers call from a number you rent from them instead.

Is it legal to call a list of phone numbers?+

It depends on who is on the list and why you are calling. In the US, sales calls to consumers must skip numbers on the National Do Not Call Registry, stay between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. in the called person's time zone, identify the caller and the business, and honor do-not-call requests within 10 business days (47 CFR 64.1200). Other countries have their own registers and hours. This is general information, not legal advice.
