Power dialer pricing: free vs paid, the honest cost math

By Vadym Berberian · Cold Call X · Updated October 20, 2026 · 9 min read

Search “free auto dialer” and you get three kinds of answers: freemium apps with hard caps, sales platforms with a free trial and a real price behind the demo wall, and comparison posts written by vendors who all happen to rank themselves #1. This guide skips the spin: what free plans actually include, what paid options really cost (with verified, dated numbers), and how to compute your own break-even as a solo caller.

What “free” dialer plans actually give you

Free tiers are real products, not scams; they are just sized so that anyone calling seriously outgrows them. Two current examples, checked October 20, 2026:

  • GoDial: free to start on the App Store, with Premium “for unlimited calling” sold as an in-app purchase at $9.99 a month, $49.99 a year, or a lifetime unlock listed at $124.99 and $139.99 (App Store listing, checked October 20, 2026).
  • Calley: a free “Personal” plan ($0.00, no expiry) capped at 25 calls a day and one active list of up to 50 records; its single-user PRO plan is $29 a month (getcalley.com pricing page, checked October 20, 2026).

What free plans typically leave out: large or unlimited lists, CSV/Sheets import with automatic mapping, filters (called/uncalled, time zone, call outcome), call outcomes and notes that persist across sessions, follow-up scheduling, and export. The dollar cost is zero; the cost in manual workarounds is not. If you spend 20 minutes a day re-copying numbers into the phone app, a free tool is costing you more than it saves.

Rule of thumb: free-plan caps rarely bite when you call occasionally from a short list. Once you dial daily from hundreds of contacts, the caps, not the features, become the bottleneck.

What paid dialers actually cost (verified, dated)

Recurring dialer pricing splits into three shapes. All figures were verified at the cited sources on their individual check dates below; pricing changes, so treat these as snapshots and confirm before purchase.

SwiftCall (iPhone) costs $14.99 on the App Store under an “AUTUMN SALE: 75% off!” banner, and its Pro subscription runs $34.99/month (both checked October 20, 2026). The same listing was $59.99 on September 28 and $9.99 behind an “over 80% off” banner in late August and September. 2. À la carte real estate platforms

Mojo charges $10/month per user for access, plus $89/month for a single-line dialer or $139 for triple-line, with recording ($25), voice ($30) and caller ID ($10) as add-ons, a solo agent realistically starts around $99–$214/month, and lead data is extra ($25–$50 a month per list type) (Mojo’s pricing page, checked October 20, 2026; details in our Mojo Dialer alternative guide).

3. Team sales platforms

PhoneBurner runs $140 / $165 / $183 per user per month billed annually (Standard / Professional / Premium), or $165 / $195 / $215 billed monthly (PhoneBurner’s pricing page, checked October 20, 2026). Full breakdown in our PhoneBurner alternative guide.

CloudTalk lists Lite, Starter and Essential at €19, €25 and €29 per user a month billed annually, where the power dialer is an add-on; it is included only in Expert, at €49 per user a month annually (€69 monthly) with a minimum of three licenses (CloudTalk’s pricing page, shown in euros at our October 20, 2026 check). One person who wants the bundled power dialer pays for three seats.

Compare the workflow before the price

A low monthly price is not automatically a low total cost. Start with what each pricing shape actually buys, then put a year on it:

  • Free tiers buy a capped list and a capped number of calls a day.
  • Per-seat subscriptions bill every month for shared records, roles, CRM routing, multi-line dialing and recording, whether one person uses them or five.
  • A one-time mobile app is paid once and works from one person’s phone and number.

Checklist before you pay anyone

  • Is the headline price the real price? Check for sale banners and expiring discounts (see SwiftCall above).
  • What does calling actually cost? VoIP minutes, DID numbers, recording and caller-ID add-ons can double a base price.
  • Per seat or per line? À la carte platforms price lines separately from users.
  • What are the free tier's caps? Contacts, calls per day, import formats.
  • Where does your lead data live? On-device vs vendor cloud is a real difference, check the privacy label, not the marketing page.
