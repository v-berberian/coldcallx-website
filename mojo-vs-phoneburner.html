How the three dial differently

  • Mojo Triple Line dials up to three numbers at once and drops the calls that don't connect — only a live answer reaches you. Mojo advertises up to 300 calls/hour. It's fast, it's aggressive, and it's the reason the person who answers sometimes hears a beat of silence. That silence is also what gets VoIP ranges flagged.
  • PhoneBurner stays on one line but removes the connection delay, then automates what happens after the call — disposition-triggered emails, SMS, and voicemail drops from its built-in CRM. It's a workflow machine for teams that live in pipelines.
  • Cold Call X walks your list one call at a time through your iPhone's native phone app — Rocket mode with no delay, or 5s/10s/randomized pacing that sounds human. You give up triple-line volume and keep: your own caller ID on every call, callbacks that ring your pocket, and the most compliance-cautious profile there is (single line, live voice, your number, only numbers you imported). Mojo vs PhoneBurner, answered

Which is cheaper, Mojo or PhoneBurner?+

Mojo's floor is lower ($99/month solo single-line vs PhoneBurner's $140 Standard), but Mojo's ceiling is higher once you add voice, recording, caller ID, and lead data — $214+/month stacks past PhoneBurner Premium's $183. Both run $1,188–$2,568 a year. Cold Call X is $12.99 once.

How much does Mojo Dialer cost per month in 2026?+

$10 Agent Access (required) + $89 Single Line or $139 Triple Line + optional Mojo Voice ($30), Call Recording ($25), Caller ID ($10), and lead data products ($25–$50 each). Solo minimum: $99/month. Verified on Mojo's pricing page, August 2026.

How much does PhoneBurner cost per month in 2026?+

PhoneBurner doesn't list prices on its site. Independent 2026 guides consistently report $140/$165/$183 per user/month billed annually (Standard/Professional/Premium), or $165/$195/$215 month-to-month, with ARMOR spam protection reportedly ~$35/seat/month extra.

What's the main difference between Mojo and PhoneBurner?+

Mojo is real-estate-native with a triple-line dialer and separate lead-data subscriptions; PhoneBurner is a single-line power dialer wrapped in a CRM with 150+ integrations for broader B2B sales teams. Both are per-seat cloud subscriptions calling from VoIP numbers.
