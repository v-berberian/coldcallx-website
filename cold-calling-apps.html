The best dialer for cold calling: three kinds compared

The best dialer for cold calling depends on who is dialing and from where. For one person working their own list from a phone, it is a power dialer: one call at a time, with you on the line, and the next lead ready when you hang up. Cold Call X is that dialer for iPhone. Here is how the three kinds of cold calling app differ.

• iPhone power dialers. A native app that dials your list through the iPhone’s own phone app, from your own number and carrier. One call at a time, with you on the line, and the next lead ready when you hang up. No VoIP line, no seat license. This is what Cold Call X is.

• Cloud power dialers. A web or desktop platform that dials through its own VoIP network, usually with a new number, call recording, team dashboards and admin controls, billed per user per month. PhoneBurner, JustCall, CloudTalk and Kixie work this way.

• Parallel and AI dialers. Cloud platforms that ring several numbers at once and connect the rep to whoever answers, built for SDR teams making hundreds of dials a day. Nooks and Orum lead with this, and CloudTalk and Kixie offer it too. It needs a cloud platform: iOS doesn’t let third-party apps place calls in the background, so no iPhone app can dial in parallel.

What to look for in a cold calling app

• Fast list import. Load a CSV or Excel export, a Google Sheet or a CRM list with columns mapped automatically, and start dialing in under a minute.

• Power dialing. The point of a cold calling app is to move through a list without retyping numbers: finish a call and the next lead is ready.

• One-tap outcomes and notes. No Answer, Voicemail, Callback, Interested: logged in a tap, with notes and last-called dates on every lead.

• Calling Hours. Leads are only dialed during their own daytime, in any time zone (legal calling hours).

• Automatic follow-ups. Each outcome should schedule the next attempt, with reminders and an exact-time alert for callbacks you book.

• CRM and Sheets sync. Import from HubSpot, Salesforce (beta) or Google Sheets, and send outcomes and notes back where they came from.

• Honest pricing. Add up the per-seat price, any seat minimum and the billing term, and compare it with a one-time cost.
