Contact us

Email support@coldcallx.app and we will get back to you, usually within one business day. You can also reach us from the app: Settings, then Contact Support.

It helps to say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you were doing when the problem came up.

What CRM X does

CRM X keeps the people you sell to, what you said to each other, and what you owe them next. Today shows who to call now, Calendar shows what is coming, Pipeline shows every deal by stage, and People holds everyone. It works on your iPhone alone: there is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

Adding people

Tap + to type someone in, pick people from your iPhone's contacts, or import a contact card or a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel). You can also open such a file from Mail, Messages or Files and choose CRM X.

Tags sort people your own way. Give someone a tag on their page, then filter People or Pipeline by it with the tag button at the top.

Next steps and reminders

Every person can have a next step with a date. When it is due they appear in Today, and after you log what happened CRM X asks for the next one. Reminders arrive as notifications if you allow them; you can change that in iOS Settings, Notifications, CRM X.

Caller ID

CRM X can label incoming calls from the people it holds with their name, company and stage. Turn it on in iOS Settings, Apps, Phone, Call Blocking & Identification, then switch on CRM X Caller ID. After adding or changing people, open CRM X once so the list is refreshed.

Backups and moving to a new iPhone

CRM X keeps backups on your iPhone and, if iCloud Drive is on, in your own iCloud. To restore, open Settings in CRM X, then Backups, and choose one. On a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account, install CRM X and restore from there.

You can also export everyone as a spreadsheet from Settings.

Working with Cold Call X

If Cold Call X is on the same iPhone, the two apps keep each other up to date: leads whose calls went somewhere appear in CRM X with their outcomes and notes, and what you log in CRM X shows on the lead card. You can send the people a tag filter shows to Cold Call X to dial through. All of it happens on your iPhone, and you can switch it off in either app's Settings.

Your data

Everything you put into CRM X stays on your iPhone. We never receive your people or notes. The app shares only anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off in Settings, Analytics & Improvement, Share Analytics. The details are in the privacy policy.

To erase everything, use Settings, Delete Everything, or delete the app.
