The short answer

For one person calling their own list on an iPhone, the simpler SwiftCall alternative is Cold Call X: a power dialer app that works through your imported list one call at a time from your own number, logs each outcome and schedules the follow-up, for $19.99 once. Who Cold Call X is for

Choose Cold Call X if you already own the list, work calls yourself, and want the number prospects call back to be your ordinary iPhone number. You get list import from a spreadsheet or your CRM, continuous dialing at your own pace, notes, one-tap call outcomes, automatic follow-ups and exact-time callback alerts, without paying every month for collaboration features you don’t use. Your leads never touch our servers.

Neither product is a compliance shortcut. You remain responsible for the source of each number, consent where required, suppression, opt-outs and calling rules. Cold Call X’s in-app Do Not Call list is a personal suppression list; it doesn’t check numbers against the National Do Not Call Registry. SwiftCall alternatives, answered

What is a good SwiftCall alternative for iPhone?+

For one person working their own list, Cold Call X is a SwiftCall alternative that costs $19.99 once with no subscription. It dials your imported list one call at a time from your own iPhone number, logs each call outcome and schedules the follow-up. Leads come from CSV, Excel, Google Sheets, HubSpot or Salesforce (beta) and stay on your iPhone.

How much does SwiftCall cost?+

SwiftCall costs $59.99 on the US App Store, and its optional SwiftCall Pro upgrade is $34.99, which the listing describes as a monthly subscription (both checked September 28, 2026). A first year with Pro comes to about $480. Earlier in 2026 the regular price was $65, often shown at $9.99 during a limited-time sale.

What does SwiftCall Pro add?+

SwiftCall's App Store listing names five Pro upgrades: cloud storage that syncs across your iCloud devices, SwiftDial rapid dialing, team collaboration through lead exporting, email and text presets, and subgroups for narrower call sessions. Pro costs $34.99 a month on top of the $59.99 app, while everything in Cold Call X is included in its $19.99 price.

Is Cold Call X cheaper than SwiftCall?+

Yes. Cold Call X is $19.99 once. SwiftCall is $59.99 up front, and one month of SwiftCall Pro ($34.99) costs more than Cold Call X does outright. Cold Call X has no paid tier: continuous dialing, call outcomes, follow-ups and CRM import are all included.

Does Cold Call X have team collaboration?+

No. Cold Call X is for one person working their own list, and your leads are stored only on your iPhone. A team can share one Google Sheet: each rep imports it on their own iPhone, and call notes are written back to the sheet.

Can I import leads into Cold Call X from a CSV or my CRM?+

Yes. Cold Call X imports CSV and Excel exports with columns mapped automatically, links Google Sheets (with call notes written back to your sheet), and imports from HubSpot and Salesforce (beta), with call outcomes and notes synced back. Leads can have several phone numbers.
