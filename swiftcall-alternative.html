A SwiftCall alternative for the simpler job.

SwiftCall is a mobile CRM for callers who want analytics, team sharing, and cloud syncing. Cold Call X is for the solo caller who wants to import a list, dial from their own iPhone number, and avoid a monthly upgrade.

The practical difference: focused dialer vs. mobile CRM

SwiftCall’s current App Store listing describes list imports, follow-up actions, dashboard analytics, and Pro features such as cloud syncing and team lead sharing. That is useful when the calling process itself needs to be shared and measured.

Cold Call X deliberately keeps the workflow narrower: bring your own CSV or Google Sheet, work one lead at a time through the iPhone Phone app, then retain the note, outcome, and callback. There is no team workspace to configure and no paid tier needed to unlock rapid list dialing.

When SwiftCall is the better fit

Choose SwiftCall if your team needs shared leads, reporting, cross-device cloud storage, or a sales CRM that tracks goals and revenue. Its App Store listing makes those priorities explicit, and they may be worth a recurring plan for a coordinated calling operation.

When Cold Call X is the better fit

Choose Cold Call X if you already own the list, work calls yourself, and want the phone number prospects call back to be your ordinary iPhone number. You get list import, pacing, notes, Hot/Warm/Cold outcomes, and calendar callbacks without paying a recurring fee for collaboration features you do not use.

Neither product is a compliance shortcut. You remain responsible for the source of each number, consent where required, suppression, opt-outs, and applicable calling rules.

SwiftCall alternatives, answered

Is Cold Call X cheaper than SwiftCall?+

Cold Call X costs $12.99 once. SwiftCall’s regular download price is $65 (vendor-confirmed August 27, 2026); its App Store listing is currently running a limited-time $9.99 sale, and the SwiftCall Pro upgrade is $34.99/month. During the sale SwiftCall’s entry price is lower, but at regular pricing Cold Call X is the cheaper download , and one month of Pro alone costs more than Cold Call X does outright.

Does Cold Call X have team collaboration?+

No. Cold Call X is for one person working their own list. A team needing shared leads, cloud sync, and reporting should choose a product designed for that workflow.

Can I import a CSV into Cold Call X?+

Yes. Cold Call X imports CSV files or Google Sheets, maps common fields, and keeps multiple phone numbers attached to one lead.
