Who Cold Call X is for

Choose Cold Call X if you already own the list, work calls yourself, and want the number prospects call back to be your ordinary iPhone number. You get list import from a spreadsheet or your CRM, continuous dialing at your own pace, notes, one-tap call outcomes, automatic follow-ups and exact-time callback alerts, without paying every month for collaboration features you don’t use. Your leads never touch our servers.

Neither product is a compliance shortcut. You remain responsible for the source of each number, consent where required, suppression, opt-outs and calling rules. Cold Call X’s in-app Do Not Call list is a personal suppression list; it doesn’t check numbers against the National Do Not Call Registry. Does Cold Call X have team collaboration?+

No. Cold Call X is for one person working their own list, and your leads are stored only on your iPhone. A team can share one Google Sheet: each rep imports it on their own iPhone, and call notes are written back to the sheet.

Can I import leads into Cold Call X from a CSV or my CRM?+

Yes. Cold Call X imports CSV and Excel exports with columns mapped automatically, links Google Sheets (with call notes written back to your sheet), and imports from HubSpot and Salesforce (beta), with call outcomes and notes synced back. Leads can have several phone numbers.
