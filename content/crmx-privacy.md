# Privacy Policy – CRM X

Effective November 17, 2026 · Version 2026-11-17

This Privacy Policy explains how Berberian Enterprises Inc. (“CRM X,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use CRM X.

## The short version

CRM X works on your iPhone, not on our servers. There is no account to create and we operate no server for it. We do not collect, receive, or store the people you add, your notes, your deals, or anything else you put into it. The App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind: it sends us nothing at all.

## Information stored on your device

The people you add or import, their contact details, companies, tags, deal values and stages, your notes and the history of what you did with each person, your next steps, saved filters, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Nothing in that data is uploaded to us. You control the contact data you add and must have the right to use it.

Reminders for your next steps are scheduled as local notifications on your device. They are not sent through any server, and the App asks for notification permission only when you choose to turn reminders on. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

When you export your people, the App creates a spreadsheet file and hands it to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes.

## Adding and importing people

You can type a person in, pick people from your iPhone's contacts, or import a contact card or spreadsheet file. Contacts are chosen in the iOS contact picker: the App receives only the people you pick and never has access to the rest of your address book. A file you import is read on your device and is not sent anywhere.

## Dictated notes

If you dictate a note, the App asks for microphone and speech recognition access and listens only while you are dictating. Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. On iPhones and languages where Apple supports it, this happens entirely on your device. Where it does not, iOS sends the audio to Apple to be transcribed, under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive the audio or the text, and the App does not keep the recording.

## Caller ID

If you turn on Caller ID for CRM X in iOS Settings, the App gives iOS the phone numbers of the people it holds, each with a name, company, and stage, so that an incoming call from one of them can be labelled. That list stays on your device and is managed by iOS. The App is never told who calls you and does not read your call history.

## Calls, texts, and email

Calls are placed by your iPhone's Phone app through your carrier. Texts and emails are handed to the messaging or mail app on your device. The App does not carry, record, or store call audio or message content. Your carrier and the apps you hand off to process that traffic under their own policies.

## Sharing with Cold Call X on the same iPhone

If Cold Call X is also installed on your iPhone, the two apps can keep each other up to date. Both are ours, and they exchange information through a private storage area on your device that only these two apps can open. Cold Call X shares the leads whose calls went somewhere, with their call outcomes, notes, and follow-up dates; CRM X shares what you log about those same people. The two apps also coordinate Caller ID so a number is labelled once. All of this happens on your device. Nothing passes through a server or leaves your iPhone, and it does nothing unless both apps are installed. You can switch the sharing off in either app's Settings.

## Backups

The App keeps automatic daily backups of your on-device data so you can restore it if something goes wrong. Unless you switch it off, the App also saves a copy of each backup to your own iCloud account, so a lost or replaced iPhone loses nothing. Those copies are held in your Apple account, not ours, in the App's private iCloud storage, and are protected by Apple's iCloud security. We cannot access them. If your device is signed out of iCloud or iCloud is off for the App, backups stay on your device only. You can turn iCloud backup off in Settings at any time.

## Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details.

## Support

If you write to us at support@coldcallx.app, we use your message and email address to respond. That correspondence is the only personal information about you that we hold, and we keep it only as long as needed to help you and to keep a record of the request.

## Retention

On-device information remains until you delete it or uninstall the App, subject to iOS backup behavior. While iCloud backup is on, backup copies remain in your iCloud account until they are replaced by newer ones or you remove them. Turning iCloud backup off stops new copies but keeps the ones already there.

## Deletion and choices

You can delete any person, note, or step in the App, or delete everything at once in Settings. You can turn off iCloud backup and sharing with Cold Call X in Settings, and reminders and Caller ID in iOS Settings. Uninstalling the App removes its on-device data. Backup copies already in your iCloud account can be removed in iOS Settings under your Apple account's iCloud storage.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to, or correction or deletion of, personal information about you that we hold, and to complain to your local privacy regulator. The only such information we hold is support correspondence; contact us at support@coldcallx.app and we will respond within a reasonable period. Data that remains only on your device or in your own iCloud account is not accessible to us, and requests concerning it must be managed using the App, your device, or your Apple account.

## Security

Your data is protected by iOS device security and, for backups in iCloud, by Apple's iCloud security. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

CRM X is not directed to children. You must be at least 18 years old to use the App.

## International processing

We are established in the United States. Because the App sends us nothing, the only information that can leave your country is support correspondence you choose to send, which we read and store in the United States.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the App or law changes. We will update the effective date and, when a change is material, provide an in-app notice where appropriate.
