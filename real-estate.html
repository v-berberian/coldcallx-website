How to choose a real estate dialer

Agents do not need the same tool as a 50-seat contact center. Before choosing software, ask four practical questions: can it import your actual seller list, can you move between calls without losing context, can you follow up when a lead says “next week,” and can you afford it at your current volume? A dialer that promises hundreds of attempts an hour is not automatically better if it creates rushed conversations, a new VoIP identity, or another CRM to maintain.

For high-volume teams, cloud platforms compete on parallel dialing, coaching, CRM sync, and reporting. For a solo agent or small investor, a focused iPhone power dialer can be the simpler fit: import the list, call one person at a time, log the outcome, and keep the same caller ID that receives the callback.

Privacy and dialing controls matter

Cold Call X is designed around numbers you imported. It does not generate or guess numbers, and calls go from your own number through your own carrier. Your lists stay on-device with encrypted iCloud backup in your own account, and we never receive them. That is a different model from a cloud dialer that stores campaigns, call records, and team activity in a vendor account.

This is not a compliance shortcut. Cold Call X includes a personal Do Not Call suppression list, but it does not scrub the National DNC Registry. Check federal and state requirements, honor opt-outs, and get legal advice for your campaign. For a broader explanation, read our auto dialer laws guide.

Real estate dialing, answered

Does Cold Call X work with skip-traced lists?+

Yes. Any skip-tracing service that exports CSV works, PropStream, BatchLeads, DealMachine, REISift, and others. Import the CSV or a Google Sheet and columns like owner name, phone numbers, and notes auto-map.

Can one seller have multiple phone numbers?+

Yes. Multi-number leads are built in, so an owner with three skip-traced numbers stays one lead instead of three duplicate rows.

Do I need a VoIP number for real estate cold calling?+

No. Cold Call X dials through your iPhone's native phone app from your own number. There is no VoIP line to rent and no per-minute fees, and your caller ID stays consistent.

Is cold calling still legal for real estate agents?+

Yes, within the rules. Federal TCPA and Do-Not-Call law (plus state versions) set permitted hours, consent, and opt-out requirements; our auto dialer laws guide walks through them in plain English. Cold Call X dials only numbers you imported, from your own number through your own carrier, and includes a personal DNC suppression list. It does not scrub the National DNC Registry, and this is not legal advice, verify current rules before dialing.

How much does a real estate dialer cost?+

It ranges from a one-time app purchase to monthly cloud seats. Cold Call X is $12.99 once, no subscription, no per-seat fees. The cloud platforms in the comparison table above charge per user per month, typically $10 in access fees plus $89–$140+ for dialer lines. Match a year of your call volume against both models before choosing.

Should a solo agent use a power dialer or a predictive dialer?+

A single-line power dialer fits most solo agents: one live conversation at a time, controlled pacing, and your own caller ID. Predictive and parallel dialers place simultaneous calls across multiple VoIP lines, impossible on one iPhone line, and suit high-volume teams chasing raw dials per hour.
