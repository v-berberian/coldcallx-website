The short answer

Cold Call X is an iPhone app that calls through a list of phone numbers for you. Import the list from a CSV file, an Excel workbook, Google Sheets, HubSpot or Salesforce (beta), tap Call, and it works down the list one number at a time. After each call you tap the outcome, and the next number is ready. It's $19.99 once, with no subscription.

The iPhone can't do this on its own. The Phone app dials one number at a time and has nowhere to put a list. Tap-to-call links and a Shortcuts loop get you through a dozen numbers, but they can't tell whether anyone answered or remind you who to call back. Our step-by-step guide on how to call a list of phone numbers on iPhone compares all four ways.

Calling a list with Cold Call X

1. Import the list. A CSV file, an Excel workbook (.xlsx, with a sheet picker when there are several tabs, since version 2.2), a Google Sheet, or HubSpot and Salesforce records (beta). Columns are mapped automatically. A lead with a mobile and an office number stays one lead with two numbers, and the list lives in the app, not in your Contacts.

2. Tap Call. Cold Call X hands the number to the iPhone's phone app, and the call goes out over your carrier from your own number. The call script, objection cards and your notes are on the lead while you talk.

3. Tap what happened. No Answer, Voicemail, Wrong Number, Callback, Interested or Not Interested, plus a note if you want one. On CSV and Google Sheets lists you can rename those outcomes or add your own, such as Appointment Set; HubSpot and Salesforce lists use the CRM's own outcomes.

4. Call the next number. It's ready as soon as you are. Tap to dial it, or turn on Auto Call and the next call starts after a countdown you choose, down to 0-second Rocket mode. You never copy, paste or retype a number.

5. Let the follow-ups schedule themselves. No Answer, Voicemail and Interested schedule the next attempt, a Callback lands at the time you book with an exact-time reminder, and Not Interested and Wrong Number end the sequence. Morning reminders and one Follow-ups list show who is due today.

If the list came from a Google Sheet, your call notes are written back to the sheet. HubSpot and Salesforce get each outcome and note on the record. Calling Hours only dials a lead during their own daytime, in any time zone.

What it doesn't do, and the rules that still apply

• One call at a time. iOS doesn't let any app place calls silently in the background, so Cold Call X never rings several numbers at once. Every call goes through the iPhone's phone app with you on the line.

• Only your own list. Cold Call X never generates or guesses phone numbers. It calls only the numbers you imported.

• Your own Do Not Call list, not the national one. Anyone you add is blocked across every list and future import, but the app doesn't check national registries such as the US National Do Not Call Registry. In the US, sales calls to consumers must skip registered numbers unless an exemption applies (47 CFR 64.1200(c)(2)), and calls to homes are limited to 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. in the called person's time zone (64.1200(c)(1)).

Calling a list on iPhone, answered

What app can call through a list of phone numbers on iPhone?+

Cold Call X. It is an iPhone app that imports a list of phone numbers from a CSV file, an Excel workbook, Google Sheets, HubSpot or Salesforce (beta) and calls through it one number at a time: tap Call, talk, tap what happened, and the next number is ready. Every call goes out through the iPhone's own phone app from your own number. It is a $19.99 one-time purchase on the US App Store, with no subscription.

How do I call a list of phone numbers on my iPhone?+

The iPhone can't do it by itself: the Phone app dials one number at a time and has no way to import a list. Tap-to-call links and a Shortcuts loop work for a handful of numbers. For a real list, import it into Cold Call X, tap Call, and after each call tap the outcome; the next number is ready straight away, and the follow-ups schedule themselves.

Can I call a list of numbers from an Excel file on my iPhone?+

Yes. Since version 2.2, Cold Call X imports Excel workbooks (.xlsx) directly, with a sheet picker when the workbook has several tabs, and maps the name and phone columns automatically. Older .xls files and password-protected workbooks need saving as .xlsx or CSV first. Columns the import doesn't map, such as Company or Role, stay on each lead and can be used in your call script.

Does it call the next number automatically?+

It can. Turn on Auto Call and the next call starts after a countdown you choose, down to 0-second Rocket mode, or leave it off and tap to dial each number. Either way, iOS places every call through the system phone app, one at a time, with you on the line. No iPhone app can ring several numbers at once or call silently in the background.

Will the numbers be added to my iPhone contacts?+

No. The list lives inside Cold Call X, not in your Contacts, so a few hundred leads don't fill up your address book or sync to your other devices. Lists, notes and call history stay on your iPhone and never reach Cold Call X servers.

How much does a call list app for iPhone cost?+

Cold Call X costs $19.99 once on the US App Store (checked November 17, 2026): no subscription, no per-minute charge and no account. Calls use your own carrier plan, so there is no VoIP line or second number to pay for.
