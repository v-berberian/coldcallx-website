Honest fit: what Cold Call X is, and isn't

Most "insurance dialer" pages sell you a call-center platform. Cold Call X is a personal tool for a desk of one, and pretending otherwise would waste your time. Here's the straight answer.

It is

  • Built for solo agents, captive agents, and independent brokers working aged leads, renewals, final expense, Medicare, and P&C lists from an iPhone.
  • Fully functional at $12.99 once, every feature, no seats, no tier to unlock.
  • Dialing from your own carrier number, so quote follow-ups and callbacks hit the phone clients already know.

It is not

  • An agency CRM, no policy pipelines, carrier downloads, or commission tracking. Lists export with notes via the iOS share sheet.
  • A call-center floor, no predictive dialing, agent leaderboards, or manager listen-in.
  • A VoIP line or lead vendor. One line, live voice, your number, and you bring the leads.

If you need a multi-agent floor with predictive dialing, coaching QA, and admin dashboards, a cloud platform is genuinely the right category, that's a different product. Insurance dialing, answered

Can I import thousands of aged leads at once?+

Yes. Cold Call X handles large CSV imports, so a full batch of aged internet leads loads in one go. Keep separate lists per lead vendor or product line, and columns auto-map on import.

Does Cold Call X dial from my own number?+

Yes. Calls go through your iPhone's native phone app from your own number, the same one on your business card and voicemail. There is no VoIP line and no per-minute cost, and callbacks come straight to you.

Is there a monthly fee per agent?+

No. Cold Call X is a one-time purchase of $12.99 on the App Store, no subscription, no per-seat licensing, and no account required.

Will dialing from my own number keep me off spam lists?+

No app can guarantee caller-ID reputation, but dialing one lead at a time from your own carrier number, the same way you'd call any client, is the least spam-like setup there is. Avoid machine-gun re-dialing the same list, and your number behaves like a normal business line.

Is cold calling aged insurance leads compliant?+

Cold Call X dials one lead at a time from your own number through the iPhone's native phone app, no recorded messages, no number generation. The rest of compliance, consent for your list, Do-Not-Call scrubbing, and calling hours by state, depends on your leads and how you call them. Read our plain-English TCPA auto dialer guide before buying aged lists.

Can multiple agents on my team use it?+

Yes. Each agent installs their own copy on their iPhone, one-time purchase per install, no per-seat subscriptions to manage. Lists, notes, and follow-ups stay on each agent's device, so nothing client-related syncs to a shared server.

How do I keep do-not-call leads from getting dialed again?+

Add a lead to the built-in do-not-call suppression list and it stays out of your dialing queue, batch after batch, even in a shuffled pass. The list manages your own opt-outs on your phone; it does not scrub the National DNC Registry, so keep scrubbing freshly purchased data at the source.

Is Cold Call X a good cold calling app for insurance brokers?+

It is built for the broker workflow: working aged lead batches and renewal books solo, from your own number, with quoted-lead tagging, timezone filters, and callback scheduling. It is not an agency CRM or a call-center floor, so it fits brokers who dial their own lists rather than teams dialing through a manager's dashboard.
