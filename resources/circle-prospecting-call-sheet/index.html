How to run a circle prospecting block with it

  1. Pick one anchor and one event. Write the address, the milestone and the date at the top of page 2. Your own listing or sale is the most credible anchor.
  2. Build a small ring and scrub it. Start with 25 to 50 nearby homes you can actually follow up with. Remove your own do-not-call list and any number on the National Do Not Call Registry, and write the scrub date on the sheet (see below).
  3. Read the script for that event once, then put it down. Say the address and the event, ask one question, and stop talking.
  4. Replace every placeholder ({Name}, {Address}, {Day}, {Neighborhood}, {Number}) with true details, and only quote sales you can name the street for.
  5. Log before the next dial. Outcome, the homeowner's exact words and the callback they agreed to. "Maybe next spring" with no date written down is a wasted call.
  6. Call the same ring at the next milestone. Under contract and just sold are new reasons to call; the best time to cold call guide covers when homeowners pick up, and the calling hours guide has the legal windows by state.

Is circle prospecting legal?

Yes, with the Do Not Call rules in force. When the National Association of Realtors asked the FCC for an exemption, the FCC held that calls by real estate agents to property owners to offer their services are telephone solicitations, while a call made only on behalf of a buyer you represent is not (FCC 05-28, paragraph 15). A nearby listing is a reason to call, not an exemption. The mechanics are in 47 CFR 64.1200: calls to homes only between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. in the homeowner's local time, a National Do Not Call Registry copy no more than 31 days old, your own internal do-not-call list, and no prerecorded voicemail without prior express written consent. The guide's calling-rules section walks through each point.

Checked October 17, 2026. This is general information, not legal advice. Talk to your broker or an attorney about your own obligations.

Which circle prospecting script should I use?+

Match the script to the event on the anchor property: the just-listed script in the first week, the open-house invitation before the open house, the under-contract script when an offer is accepted and the just-sold script after closing. With no listing of your own, use the market-update script, and only quote neighborhood numbers you pulled from your MLS that morning. Each one is on page 1 of the sheet.

How many times can I call the same circle?+

Once per real milestone on the same listing: just listed, open house, under contract and just sold, which is four honest reasons over a couple of months. Every call needs its own reason, and anyone who asks not to be called comes off the list permanently, whichever touch they said no on.
