What’s in 2.0

Everything between the list and the deal, on the phone in your pocket.

  • Continuous dialing

    Finish a call and the next lead is ready, at the pace you choose, from your own number. How the dialer works

  • One-tap call outcomes

    No Answer, Voicemail, Wrong Number, Callback, Interested, Not Interested. Each one schedules the next attempt for you.

  • Follow-ups that plan themselves

    Morning reminders, an exact-time alert for every callback you book, and one Follow-ups list across all your lists.

  • HubSpot, Salesforce and Google Sheets

    Import from your CRM or your sheet. Outcomes and notes sync back to HubSpot and Salesforce (beta), and notes back to your Google Sheet.

  • Calling Hours

    Leads are only dialed during their own daytime, in any time zone. Works with US, Canadian, UK and Australian numbers. Legal calling hours

  • Goals and list stats

    A daily dial goal and streaks that count only real calls, plus connect rate, outcomes and the best time to call for every list.

  • Scripts and objections

    Your call script and objection cards on every lead, and text templates for after a voicemail, after no answer, or a confirmed callback.

  • A Do Not Call list that sticks

    Block a contact once and they stay blocked across every list and every future import.

  • Private by design

    No account, no ads, no subscription. Your leads stay on your iPhone and never touch our servers, and iCloud backups are encrypted on your device.
