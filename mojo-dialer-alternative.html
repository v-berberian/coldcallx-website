Where dialers like Mojo sting

None of this makes Mojo a bad product; it's a workhorse for call-center teams. But most individual agents and investors never use the machinery they're paying for:

  • The VoIP number problem. Cloud dialers call from numbers they issue you. Those ranges get flagged as spam or telemarketing by carriers, which is exactly why Mojo sells Caller ID with "attestation" as an add-on. Your own iPhone number doesn't have that problem.
  • Seats you don't have. Per-user and per-license pricing is built for teams. Solo pros pay team infrastructure rates.
  • Onboarding and accounts. Accounts, billing, provisioning, admin portals: overhead that exists to serve the platform, not your next call.
  • The meter never stops. Skip a week of prospecting and the subscription bills anyway. A dialer you own outright never bills you for a slow month.

Mojo alternatives, answered

How much does Mojo Dialer cost per month?+

Mojo charges à la carte: $10/user Agent Access, $89/license Single Line or $139/license Triple Line Dialer, plus optional Mojo Voice ($30), Call Recording ($25), and Caller ID ($10). Solo single-line starts at $99/month; a loaded triple-line setup runs $214/month or more. Prices as published on Mojo's pricing page in September 2026.

What is the cheapest alternative to Mojo Dialer?+

For solo prospectors, Cold Call X: $12.99 once, no monthly fee, no per-minute VoIP charges. It dials your lists through your iPhone's native phone app from your own number.

Does Cold Call X dial three lines at once?+

No, single-line power dialing by design. It advances through your list automatically, one call at a time, from your own carrier line. That keeps your caller ID clean and every conversation human. Teams that need simultaneous triple-line dialing may still prefer Mojo.

Can I move my lead lists out of Mojo?+

Yes. Export from Mojo's Lead Manager as CSV and import into Cold Call X: columns auto-map, and your data lives on your device from then on.

Why dial from my own number instead of a VoIP number?+

VoIP dialer ranges get spam-flagged by carriers; that's why cloud dialers sell caller-ID reputation as an add-on. Your own number carries trust, and callbacks reach your pocket directly.

Is Cold Call X a subscription like Mojo?+

No. Cold Call X is a one-time $12.99 purchase on the App Store: no account, no recurring billing, no per-seat fees. Mojo bills monthly for each user or license, plus optional add-ons like Mojo Voice ($30) and Call Recording ($25), so the cost recurs whether or not you dial.

What do I give up by switching from Mojo to Cold Call X?+

Mojo's lead data subscriptions, team seats, supervisor listening, triple-line dialing, and cloud reporting stay Mojo-only. Cold Call X is a single-line, solo list dialer: you call from your own number, your leads stay on your iPhone, and you pay once. If you run a call-center-style team, Mojo still fits better.
