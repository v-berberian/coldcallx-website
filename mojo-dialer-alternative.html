Where dialers like Mojo sting

None of this makes Mojo a bad product; it's a workhorse for call-center teams. But most individual agents and investors never use the machinery they're paying for:

  • The VoIP number problem. Cloud dialers call from numbers they issue you. Those ranges get flagged as spam or telemarketing by carriers, which is exactly why Mojo sells Caller ID with "attestation" as an add-on. Your own iPhone number doesn't have that problem.
  • Seats you don't have. Per-user and per-license pricing is built for teams. Solo pros pay team infrastructure rates.
  • Onboarding and accounts. Accounts, billing, provisioning, admin portals: overhead that exists to serve the platform, not your next call.
  • The meter never stops. Skip a week of prospecting and the subscription bills anyway. A dialer you own outright never bills you for a slow month.

Mojo alternatives, answered

How much does Mojo Dialer cost per month?+

Mojo charges à la carte, month to month: $10/user Agent Access (required), $89/license for the Single Line Dialer or $139/license for the Triple Line Dialer, plus optional Mojo Voice ($30), Call Recording ($25) and Mojo Caller ID ($10). Lead data is extra: Expired leads $50, FSBO $25, For Rent By Owner $25, Skip Tracer $49, Pre-Foreclosures $49 and Neighborhood Search $49 a month. A solo agent on the single-line dialer pays at least $99/month; single-line plus expired leads is $149/month; a loaded triple-line setup runs $214/month before any lead data. Prices as published on mojosells.com/pricing, checked September 27, 2026.

Does Mojo Dialer have a free trial?+

Mojo's pricing and FAQ pages don't mention a free trial (checked September 27, 2026). Mojo bills monthly, prepaid, with no contract, setup fee or cancellation penalty, so the cheapest way to test it is one month of Agent Access plus the Single Line Dialer: $99. Cold Call X has no trial either; it is a one-time $19.99 App Store purchase with no subscription.

Does Mojo have an iPhone app?+

Yes. Mojo On the Go is Mojo's iOS and Android app, which Mojo calls a power dialer in your pocket. It is a companion to a Mojo account, and Mojo's mobile-apps page doesn't list a separate price, so budget for the monthly licenses. Cold Call X is a standalone iPhone power dialer: no account, $19.99 once, and it dials through the iPhone's own phone app from your own number.

What is the cheapest alternative to Mojo Dialer?+

For solo agents and investors who just need to work a list fast, Cold Call X is the cheapest real option: a one-time $19.99 purchase on the App Store with no monthly fee. It dials through your iPhone's native phone app from your own number, so there are also no per-minute VoIP charges. It carries a 4.8-out-of-5 App Store rating (67 ratings, September 2026), there is no account to create, and your lists and notes stay stored locally on your device.

Does Cold Call X dial three lines at once like Mojo's Triple Line Dialer?+

No. Cold Call X is a single-line power dialer by design: it advances through your list automatically, one call at a time, through your own carrier line. That keeps your personal caller ID on every call, avoids the spam tagging that VoIP numbers attract, and keeps you compliant and human on every conversation.

Can I move my lead lists out of Mojo into Cold Call X?+

Yes. Export your leads from Mojo's Lead Manager as CSV, then import the file into Cold Call X: columns auto-map, and you can keep separate lists per market or lead source. Your data lives on your device, not in a subscription account.

Why is dialing from my own number better than a VoIP dialer number?+

VoIP and cloud dialer numbers are frequently flagged as spam or telemarketing by carriers, which crushes answer rates. Calls from your own iPhone number carry the caller ID prospects may already know: your business card, your voicemail, your texts; and when someone calls back, it rings the phone in your pocket instead of a web app.

Is Cold Call X a subscription like Mojo?+

No. Cold Call X is a one-time $19.99 purchase on the App Store: no account, no recurring billing, no per-seat fees. Mojo bills monthly for each user or license, plus optional add-ons like Mojo Voice ($30) and Call Recording ($25), so the cost recurs whether or not you dial.

What do I give up by switching from Mojo to Cold Call X?+

Mojo's lead data subscriptions, team seats, supervisor listening, triple-line dialing, and cloud reporting stay Mojo-only. Cold Call X is a single-line, solo list dialer: you call from your own number, your leads stay on your iPhone, and you pay once. If you run a call-center-style team, Mojo still fits better.
