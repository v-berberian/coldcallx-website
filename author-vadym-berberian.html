Vadym Berberian

Vadym Berberian is the founder of Cold Call X, an iPhone power dialer and mobile CRM for people working their own outreach lists. His published guides focus on practical calling workflows for real-estate, mortgage, and insurance brokers, including list organization, follow-up, and compliant dialing habits.

He reviews product facts against the App Store listing and checks time-sensitive competitor pricing against publicly available first-party sources where possible. The guides are educational: they do not replace a broker’s compliance process, legal advice, or the terms of a lead provider.
