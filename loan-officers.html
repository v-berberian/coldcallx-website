Honest fit: what Cold Call X is, and isn't

Most "mortgage dialer" pages sell you a call-center platform wired into a CRM. This is a focused prospecting companion for an individual originator, not a replacement for the systems that run a mortgage operation. Here is the practical boundary.

It is

  • Built for solo loan officers and independent brokers working partner lists, past-client databases, and aged lead batches from an iPhone.
  • The full feature set is available for a single $12.99 purchase, without branch seats or feature tiers.
  • Dialing from your own carrier number, so partner call-backs hit the phone they already have.

It is not

  • A mortgage CRM or LOS, no loan pipelines, pre-qual tracking, or document collection. Working data can leave the app through the iOS share sheet when you need to update your approved pipeline records.
  • A call-center floor, no predictive dialing, agent leaderboards, or speed-to-lead automation.
  • A VoIP line or lead vendor. It is one live conversation at a time, using your number and your approved contact source.

If you need a team dialer with predictive pacing, CRM sync, and speed-to-lead routing, a cloud platform is genuinely the right category, that's a different product. Loan officer dialing, answered

Can I import my lists from a CSV export?+

Yes. Export the fields you need from your LOS, spreadsheet, or contact database, then import the CSV or Google Sheet. Keep partner referrals, past clients, and purchased leads in separate lists so the source and follow-up context remain clear.

Does Cold Call X dial from my own number?+

Yes. The call hands off to the iPhone phone app and uses your carrier number, so realtor partners and past clients see the number they already recognize. Cold Call X does not add a VoIP seat or per-minute calling charge.

Is there a monthly fee or per-seat license?+

No. The App Store purchase is $12.99 once. There is no recurring subscription or account setup for the app.

Is cold calling mortgage leads compliant?+

The app places one call at a time from your iPhone and does not generate numbers or play recorded messages. Whether a campaign is permitted still depends on consent, Do-Not-Call status, state calling hours, and whether you are contacting a consumer or a business. Review our TCPA guide and get advice for your campaign; this is not legal advice.

Will it replace my mortgage CRM?+

No. Keep applications, disclosures, milestones, and borrower documents in your LOS or mortgage CRM. Cold Call X handles the calling list, notes, tags, and reminders, and you can export data through the iOS share sheet.

Can multiple loan officers on my team use it?+

Each officer uses their own iPhone installation and keeps their working lists and notes on that device. There is no shared team workspace or per-seat subscription, so a branch should define its own process for approved lists, records, and follow-up handoffs.
