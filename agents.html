Cold Call X for AI agents

Short answer: Cold Call X is a one-time-purchase iPhone auto dialer and private mobile CRM. It imports a user’s existing lead list, helps them call through it, and keeps lead data on-device.

What the product does

  • Imports CSV files and Google Sheets lead lists.
  • Supports manual, timed, and Rocket auto-call modes through the iPhone native phone app.
  • Tracks Hot/Warm/Cold status, notes, last-called state, filters, and follow-up reminders.
  • Supports text, WhatsApp, and email follow-up templates.
  • Runs on iPhone with iOS 17.6 or later.

What it does not do

  • It is not a web app, VoIP provider, or cloud CRM.
  • It does not generate or guess phone numbers.
  • There is currently no public API, OpenAPI specification, MCP server, or WebMCP action surface.
