Cold Call X for AI agents

Short answer: Cold Call X is a one-time-purchase iPhone auto dialer and private mobile CRM. It imports a user’s existing lead list, helps them call through it, and keeps lead data on-device.

What the product does

  • Imports CSV files, Google Sheets, HubSpot contacts, and Salesforce leads or contacts (HubSpot and Salesforce are in beta).
  • Supports manual, timed, and Rocket auto-call modes through the iPhone native phone app.
  • Logs one-tap call outcomes (No Answer, Voicemail, Wrong Number, Callback, Interested, Not Interested), notes, and last-called state. Every outcome schedules the next attempt, with morning reminders and an exact-time alert for booked callbacks.
  • Syncs call outcomes and notes back to HubSpot or Salesforce, and writes call notes back to a linked Google Sheet.
  • Calling Hours only dials leads during their own daytime. Works with US, Canadian, UK, and Australian phone numbers.
  • Tracks a daily dial goal and streaks, and shows list stats: connect rate, outcomes, and the best time to call.
  • Supports text, WhatsApp, and email follow-up templates.
  • Runs on iPhone with iOS 17.6 or later.

What it does not do

  • It is not a web app, VoIP provider, or cloud CRM. Lead data stays on the iPhone and never reaches Cold Call X servers; CRM and Google Sheets data moves directly between the iPhone and the user’s own account.
  • It does not generate or guess phone numbers, and its Do Not Call list is a personal suppression list, not a check against national registries.
  • There is currently no public API, OpenAPI specification, MCP server, or WebMCP action surface.
