What PhoneBurner actually costs in 2026

PhoneBurner doesn't publish plan prices on its marketing pages, but 2026 pricing guides that track it closely put the entry Standard plan around $140 per user/month billed annually, with higher tiers running to $183+/user. Outbound SMS beyond plan limits costs extra. For one rep, that's $1,680 to $2,200 a year — every year. Cold Call X is $12.99, once, total.

To be fair, that money buys real machinery: a built-in CRM, 150+ integrations, team admin and call coaching. The question is whether a solo prospector uses any of it.

The features solo reps pay for and never open

  • A CRM you already have. If your leads live in a spreadsheet, a CSV, or Google Sheets, you don't need a second CRM with a per-user seat — Cold Call X imports your list and keeps notes, tags, and call history on the lead itself.
  • Integrations for teams. Salesforce and HubSpot sync, admin portals, live call monitoring — that's team infrastructure. Solo prospecting needs a fast dialer and follow-up reminders, not middleware.
  • Voice infrastructure. Cloud dialer minutes are a real cost — which is why they're a real line item on your invoice. Your iPhone already has unlimited minutes with a caller ID prospects trust.
  • Onboarding calls. When the platform is complicated, "expert onboarding" is a feature. When it's a dialer you already know how to use, it's overhead.

What Cold Call X keeps

The point of a power dialer is simple: more conversations per hour, zero numbers dialed by hand, nothing forgotten afterward. Cold Call X does exactly that, on-device:

  • One-tap advance through your list. Import a CSV or Google Sheet, auto-map columns, and dial — no number is ever typed by hand.
  • Your own number out. Calls go through your iPhone's native phone app. No VoIP number to get spam-flagged, no per-minute meter.
  • Disposition + follow-up. Tag Hot/Warm/Cold mid-call, jot notes, and schedule callbacks as iOS calendar events before the next call connects.
  • Timezone-aware. Filter lists by timezone so you're always calling at a sane hour — the cheapest answer-rate boost there is.
  • No account, no lock-in. Your lead data stays on your device and exports whenever you want it.
