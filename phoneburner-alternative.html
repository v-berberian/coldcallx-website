The features solo reps pay for and never open

  • A CRM you already have. If your leads live in a spreadsheet, a CSV, or Google Sheets, you don't need a second CRM with a per-user seat: Cold Call X imports your list and keeps notes, call outcomes, and call history on the lead itself.
  • Integrations for teams. Admin portals, live call monitoring, Zapier-style middleware; that's team infrastructure. Solo prospecting needs a fast dialer and follow-up reminders, and if your contacts live in HubSpot or Salesforce, Cold Call X 2.0 syncs outcomes and notes back to them (beta).
  • Voice infrastructure. Cloud dialer minutes are a real cost, which is why they're a real line item on your invoice. Your iPhone already has unlimited minutes with a caller ID prospects trust.
  • Onboarding calls. When the platform is complicated, "expert onboarding" is a feature. When it's a dialer you already know how to use, it's overhead.

What Cold Call X keeps

The point of a power dialer is simple: more conversations per hour, zero numbers dialed by hand, nothing forgotten afterward. Cold Call X does exactly that, on-device:

  • One-tap advance through your list. Import a CSV or Google Sheet, auto-map columns, and dial. No number is ever typed by hand.
  • Your own number out. Calls go through your iPhone's native phone app. No VoIP number to get spam-flagged, no per-minute meter.
  • Disposition + follow-up. Tap the outcome as you hang up, jot notes, and book callbacks with an exact-time reminder before the next call connects.
  • Calling Hours. Leads are only dialed during their own daytime, so you're always calling at a sane hour, the cheapest answer-rate boost there is.
  • No account, no lock-in. Your lead data stays on your device and exports whenever you want it.

PhoneBurner alternatives, answered

How much does PhoneBurner cost per month?+

PhoneBurner's pricing page (checked September 27, 2026) lists three plans per user per month: Standard $140 billed annually or $165 monthly, Professional $165 or $195, and Premium $183 or $215. Every plan includes unlimited dialing to the US and Canada; SMS is only on Premium (1,000 outbound a month, then $15 per extra 1,000). For one person that's $1,680 to $2,580 a year.

What is a cheap alternative to PhoneBurner for one person?+

For one person who calls from an iPhone, Cold Call X: a power dialer app that works through your imported list one call at a time from your own number, with one-tap outcomes, notes and automatic follow-ups, for $19.99 once. PhoneBurner's cheapest plan is $140 per user a month billed annually ($165 month to month). You give up team features such as live call monitoring, lead distribution and PhoneBurner's built-in CRM.

Does PhoneBurner have a free trial or a contract?+

PhoneBurner offers a free trial without a credit card, with free minutes to test the dialer, and says it requires no contract: billing is month to month, with 15% off for paying annually. Its FAQ also says it dials only numbers in the United States and Canada (checked September 27, 2026).

What's the best PhoneBurner alternative for solo reps?+

If you prospect alone from your iPhone, Cold Call X does the core job: advancing through an imported call list with one-tap outcomes, notes, and automatic follow-ups, for a one-time $19.99 instead of a monthly per-user subscription. You give up multi-user CRM features you probably don't use as a solo. Cold Call X holds a 4.7-out-of-5 App Store rating (70 ratings, October 2026): no account required, with your lists and notes stored locally on your device.

Does PhoneBurner call from my own number?+

PhoneBurner is a cloud dialer: its platform places the calls, and you connect to it with your phone or, on Professional and up, a browser softphone. Cold Call X calls through your iPhone's native phone app from your own number, so callbacks hit your pocket directly.

Can I export my contacts out of PhoneBurner?+

Yes, export as CSV and import into Cold Call X with auto-mapped columns. Your list lives on your device afterward, never behind a subscription.

Is there a free PhoneBurner alternative?+

Genuinely free power dialers don't really exist: freemium plans cap contacts, daily dials, or features until you pay, and PhoneBurner's own free trial (no credit card, a limited number of free minutes) ends in a paid seat. Cold Call X is $19.99 once with no per-user fee, and it dials your full imported list without caps. For the honest math, see our free vs paid power dialer cost guide.

What do I give up by switching from PhoneBurner to Cold Call X?+

Team machinery: shared seats, lead distribution, admin dashboards, recording, and call coaching stay with PhoneBurner. What you keep is the part that books meetings: fast list advancing from your own number, one-tap outcomes, notes, and follow-ups, with your data stored on your device. If you work from HubSpot or Salesforce, Cold Call X 2.0 imports from both and logs each call's outcome and notes back (beta).
